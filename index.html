Experimental

Automatic waypoint suggestions

Automatic waypoint suggestions are an experimental feature introduced in 2.2.0-dev.x, designed primarily to work with content added by other mods without requiring manual configuration.

When you press the V key while looking at a block or creature, the mod attempts to detect the target and create a waypoint with an appropriate name, icon and position.

This feature is still under development, so some matches may be inaccurate.

Compatibility

Designed for modded content.

The automatic suggestion system uses technical identifiers and localization data provided by the game and installed mods. Its goal is to recognize modded creatures and blocks without requiring separate configuration for every mod.
